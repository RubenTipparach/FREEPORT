Freeport hex terrain

The same planet, sea, field and seed as tenebris would draw it: a Goldberg polyhedron, one column per tile in one metre blocks. The same three towns, but here a town is tiles levelled to one height and a building is a stack of concrete hex blocks, windows lit by the shader. Walk it.

What to judge on foot, against the marching cubes mockup on the same seed:
  • every hill is a stair: a metre block is a jump, a half metre kerb is a step, and a beach is a terrace
  • the town's level sits in the hillside as a cut, with a wall wherever the hill was higher
  • a building is the ground grown up: nothing to place, nothing to align, and every wall is on the grid
  • the pentagons, and what a hex footprint does to a street corner
