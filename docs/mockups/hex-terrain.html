RAISING THE COLUMNS

Freeport hex terrain

The same planetoid, the same field and seed, as tenebris would draw it: a Goldberg polyhedron (an icosahedron subdivided and dualled, twelve pentagons and the rest hexagons), one column per tile at the height the field first turns to rock, quantised to one metre blocks, with walls wherever a neighbour stands lower. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

What to look at, against the marching cubes mockup on the same seed:
  • the same hills as terraces: readable, buildable, and never an overhang
  • the twelve pentagons, which a tile grid on a sphere cannot avoid
  • the walls, which are the whole cost: a flat plain is cheap and a cliff is not
  • what a metre block reads as at a walker's eye height
