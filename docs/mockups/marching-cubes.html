Freeport marching cubes

A planet with a sea, sandy shores and green land as a density field, marched on the classic tables the core carries. Three towns stand on ground the field itself was flattened under, built from a block kit: slabs, wall panels, glass, pillars, parapets, gables, a doorway on the street, a floor a storey, a flight of stairs along a wall, a lamp on every ceiling. Walk in.

What to judge on foot, against the hex mockup on the same seed:
  • a slope is a slope: you walk up a hill and down to the beach without a step anywhere, and a cliff stops you
  • the town's plateau meets the hillside in a smooth skirt, cut by the field rather than laid on it
  • buildings stand plumb on their own patch of a curved world; walk through the door, up the stairs, floor by floor, in the sun's shadow and the lamps' light
  • the shore: wet sand at the water line, and the sea holds you at wading depth
