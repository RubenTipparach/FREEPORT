MARCHING THE FIELD

Freeport marching cubes

A planetoid as a density field and the classic mesher on the classic tables, the same tables freeport_core carries. The ground is a smooth surface with overhangs and undercuts where the field says so. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

What to look at, against the hex mockup on the same seed:
  • the lip of every crater and the ledges the volumetric term cuts: a hex column cannot lean out
  • the silhouette against the stars from orbit, which is a curve here and a staircase there
  • the same three materials on the same slopes, so only the mesher differs
  • the triangle count for the same ground, because that is what the GPU pays
